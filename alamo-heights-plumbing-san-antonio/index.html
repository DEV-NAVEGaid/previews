Leak repair

Find the leak, fix it properly

Water stains, a high bill or the sound of dripping inside a wall all point to a leak. We locate the source, repair or replace the damaged pipe or fitting, and pressure-test the line before we leave. Most repairs are finished in one same-day visit, and we quote the price in writing before any work starts.

Why choose our leak repair

  • Same-day visits – we aim to be at your door the day you call.
  • Upfront pricing – you approve the written quote before we start.
  • Tested repairs – every line is pressure-tested before we pack up.

What's included

Leak detection and diagnosis

Pipe or fitting repair

Pressure test after repair

Water heater installation

Hot water that keeps up

When a water heater fails, you need an answer fast. We help you choose between tank and tankless units, remove the old heater, install the new one to code and test the connections. Most installations are completed in a single visit, and the price is agreed in writing before we begin.

Why choose our installations

  • Clear options – we explain tank and tankless costs in plain terms.
  • Single-visit installs – most heaters are replaced the same day.
  • Written warranty – parts and labor are covered in writing.

What's included

Removal of the old unit

Drain cleaning

Clear drains, lasting flow

Slow sinks, gurgling pipes and backed-up tubs usually mean a blockage that will return unless it is cleared properly. We use cable machines and, when needed, a camera to see the line, remove the clog and check for damage. You get a written quote before we start.

Why choose our drain cleaning

  • Camera on request – we show you what is in the line.
  • Right tool for the clog – cable for roots and grease, not guesswork.
  • Honest advice – we tell you if a repair is needed instead of repeat cleanings.

What's included

Cable or jetting clearing

Camera inspection on request

Fixture and trap check

Advice to prevent repeat clogs
