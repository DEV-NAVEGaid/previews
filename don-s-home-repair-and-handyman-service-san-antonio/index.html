Home repair

What home repair covers

Fixing what has worn, leaked, cracked or come loose.

Houses in San Antonio deal with heat, shifting soil and sudden storms, and small defects grow quickly if they are left alone. We repair damaged drywall, rotted trim, sticking doors, loose fascia, cracked caulking and failing fixtures. Each job starts with a look at the cause, so the repair lasts instead of simply covering the damage.

Why homeowners choose it

  • Clear estimates. You know the price before work starts.
  • Lasting repairs. We fix the cause, not only the visible damage.
  • Clean finish. Surfaces are patched, sanded and painted to match.

Handyman services

What handyman services cover

The small jobs that pile up and never reach the top of the list.

One visit can clear a whole list: assembling furniture, mounting shelves and televisions, hanging blinds and mirrors, replacing light fixtures and door hardware, caulking, weatherstripping and minor adjustments around the house. We bring the tools, sort the order of work and keep a running tally, so you see what was done and what it cost.

Why homeowners choose it

  • One visit, many jobs. Bundle your list into a single appointment.
  • Straight pricing. Hourly and package rates are explained up front.
  • Careful with your home. Floors covered and walls protected.
