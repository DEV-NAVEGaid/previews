Lawn care

A steady routine for a healthy lawn

Lawn care covers the regular work that keeps grass even, edges sharp and beds tidy. We cut to a height that suits your grass type, trim along walls and fences, and blow clippings off paths and driveways.

Power washing

Clean surfaces without damage

Dirt, algae and mildew build up quickly on San Antonio driveways, patios and fences. We use the right pressure and nozzle for each surface so they come back clean without being scarred.

Washing works well as a one-time refresh before an event or alongside a monthly lawn plan. We give you a price by phone before booking.

Why choose our power washing

  • Right pressure – matched to concrete, pavers, brick or wood.
  • Even results – no stripes or missed corners.
  • Clean-up included – runoff directed away and debris cleared.
