How it works

Trim. Light. Let it pool.

Three small habits for an even burn and a longer life for every jar.

  1. 1

    Trim

    Cut the wick to ¼ inch before every light.

  2. 2

    Light

    Burn for 2–3 hours the first time so the wax melts edge to edge.

  3. 3

    Rest

    Snuff the flame, let the wax set and keep the jar away from drafts.

Our story

About us

A San Antonio studio pouring candles for tables, weddings and slow evenings.

Our beginning

How it started

It began with a borrowed stockpot in a South Side kitchen and one question: could a candle smell like the Hill Country in April? Friends asked for more jars. A studio on South Alamo followed, and the pouring has never stopped.

Our process

How we make it

From the wax delivery to your doorstep, in four steps.

  1. 01

    Source

    Soy wax from a Midwest farm co-op and oils from trusted suppliers.

  2. 02

    Make

    Wax is melted, scented and poured at the right temperature.

  3. 03

    Check

    Each batch cures for a week and gets a test burn.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Packed by hand for pickup on Alamo St or shipping.
