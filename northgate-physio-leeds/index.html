Physiotherapy assessment

A full picture before treatment

Your first appointment is a 45-minute assessment with a physiotherapist. We ask about your symptoms, work and activity, test how you move and explain what we find in plain words. You leave with a diagnosis, a clear plan and your first exercises. Most people need three to six sessions, and we tell you that at the start.

Why book an assessment

  • Clear diagnosis – you know what is causing the pain and why.
  • Realistic plan – sessions, timeline and goals agreed together.
  • Early relief – hands-on treatment starts in the same session.

What's included

  • 45-minute one-to-one appointment
  • Movement and strength testing
  • Written summary of findings
  • First home exercise plan

Sports massage

Deep tissue work for active people

Our sports massage sessions focus on tight, overworked muscles from training, physical jobs or long hours at a desk. We use firm, targeted pressure, explain what we feel as we go and adjust to what is comfortable for you. Sessions last 45 minutes and suit runners, cyclists, gym users and anyone carrying tension.

Why choose sports massage

  • Faster recovery – less soreness between training sessions.
  • Easier movement – looser muscles and a fuller range of motion.
  • Early warning – we spot problem areas before they become injuries.

What's included

  • 45-minute massage session
  • Check of your training load
  • Stretching advice to take home
  • Referral to a physio if needed

Back and neck pain treatment

Relief for back and neck pain

Back and neck pain are the most common reasons people come to see us. Most cases improve well with the right mix of hands-on treatment, gentle movement and small changes to posture and routine. We look at how you sit, sleep and work, then build a plan that eases pain now and lowers the chance of it returning.

Why choose this treatment

  • Less pain – mobilisation and soft tissue work ease stiffness.
  • More confidence – you learn which movements are safe and helpful.
  • Lasting change – desk, sleep and lifting advice that fits your day.

What's included

  • Spine and posture assessment
  • Hands-on mobilisation
  • Desk and sleep set-up advice
  • Progressive exercise plan

Post-surgery rehabilitation

Rehabilitation after surgery

After an operation, steady and well-planned rehabilitation makes a real difference to how you recover. We work alongside your surgeon's guidance for knee, hip, shoulder and spinal procedures, starting gently and building strength week by week. Every stage has a clear goal, from walking comfortably to returning to work, sport or daily routines.

Why choose rehabilitation with us

  • Safe progress – exercises matched to each stage of healing.
  • Regained strength – structured loading to rebuild muscle.
  • Back to routine – goals set around your work, home and hobbies.

What's included

  • Review of your surgical notes
  • Staged strength programme
  • Scar and swelling management
  • Home visit option early on

Home visits

Physiotherapy in your own home

If travelling to the clinic is difficult, we bring physiotherapy to you. Home visits suit people recovering from surgery or a fall, those with limited mobility and anyone who prefers treatment at home. We cover Leeds and nearby areas, bring portable equipment and look at how you move around your own space.

Why choose a home visit

  • No travel – treatment where you are most comfortable.
  • Real-life practice – stairs, chairs and steps in your own home.
  • Family involved – carers can join and learn how to help.

What's included

  • 50-minute visit in your home
  • Portable treatment equipment
  • Falls and home safety check
  • Exercise plan for your space
