Our journey

Dentistry that puts you at ease

We started with a simple idea: a local practice where nobody feels rushed or judged. That is still how we work today.

Vision

A Kirkstall where every neighbour can look after their teeth without fear or surprise bills.

Mission

Milestones

How the practice has grown

A few moments that shaped the way we care for our patients.

2012

Opening on Abbey Road

We welcomed our first patients to a small, friendly practice in Kirkstall.

2016

Hygienist appointments

Adding a hygienist let us put gum health at the heart of everyday care.

2020

Emergency time each day

We began keeping time free for patients in pain who need to be seen quickly.
